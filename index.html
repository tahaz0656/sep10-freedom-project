Part A

    Droid Tesla:

  • This app is the only electronic circuit simulator for android, since it solves basic circuits using Kirkchoff's Current Law (KCL).
  • This simulator forms a matrix according to KCL and then starts to solve for the unknown quantities using algebraic technologies.
  • The app is also free and also suitable for all types of people like students, hobbyists, and professionals.

    • Engineering Unit Converter & Calculator:

  • This app is a tool that converts measurements between different units such as meters to miles and Pascals to Psi.
  • Easy to read text with an intelligent, customizable display systems.
  • This tool is essential for handling complex calculations and avoid mistakes.

    • Cyberknife

  • This device/system is a non-invasive treatment for cancerous and non-cancerous tumors and other conditions where radiation therapy is indicated.
  • This device/system is used for treating conditions throughout the body, including the prostate, lung, brain, spine, head and neck, liver, pancreas and kidney.
  • The CyberKnife System is the only radiation delivery system that features a linear accelerator (linac) and is directly set on a robot to deliver the high-energy x-rays or photons used in radiation therapy.
  • It has also enabled stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS) and stereotactic body radiation therapy (SBRT) treatments for the full range of tumor types.
  • The advanced motion synchronization technology eliminates the need to use uncomfortable patient restraints, or ask patients to hold their breath for example.
